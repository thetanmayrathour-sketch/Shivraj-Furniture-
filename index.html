About Shivraj Furniture & Mobile Shop

Established in 1993 in the heart of Lucknow, Shivraj has grown from a small family business into a landmark destination for premium furniture and cutting-edge mobile technology.

With over three decades of experience, we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern innovation. Every piece of furniture is carefully selected for durability and elegance. Every smartphone and accessory is 100% authentic and comes with full manufacturer warranty.
